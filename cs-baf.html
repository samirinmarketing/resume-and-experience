Bangladesh Air Force · Social media · Government

Social media for two official Air Force pages

One page recruits young officers. The other sells museum visits. Both belong to the Bangladesh Air Force, so both had to sound official and still work on a feed built for fun. Done with Techabyte Solutions.

At a glance

  • Two pages, two briefs: civic pride for recruitment, a great day out for the museum.
  • Bangla and English copy, so nobody gets left out.
  • Reel scripts for online ticket booking and virtual museum walk-throughs.

01 / 04

The problem

Recruitment needed eligible young people to apply. That means aspirational and clear, not stiff or preachy.

The museum needed followers to become ticket buyers. That means fun, educational and worth the trip.

Both had to keep an official voice on a platform that rewards entertainment.

02 / 04

The plan

  • Recruitment: aimed at 18–25 year olds. Service framed as a career, a duty and a chance to grow.
  • Museum: a living history day out, not a static exhibit. Real aircraft, real stories, and tickets you can book in one click.
  • Both: Bangla and English, to reach every audience.
03 / 04

What I did

  • Ongoing captions for both pages.
  • Recruitment posts: eligibility highlights, deadline reminders, career path stories.
  • Museum posts: visit promos, events, features on aircraft and Air Force milestones.
  • Reel scripts for online tickets and virtual walk-throughs.
  • National day, Air Force anniversary and Independence Day campaigns.
