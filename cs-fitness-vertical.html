The problem

"Scheduling plugin" is a crowded search with hundreds of products. Gyms, studios and trainers search differently, with terms like "fitness class booking WordPress", and WP Timetics had nothing for them.

They have their own needs too: class schedules, recurring sessions, trainer assignment and capacity limits. Generic scheduling content skips all of that.
