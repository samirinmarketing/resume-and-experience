Vertical Targeting: Fitness Businesses

Four sequential documents narrowing WP Timetics from "scheduling plugin for everyone" to "the booking tool for fitness and gym businesses." Going deep instead of staying horizontal.

The Problem 01 / 04

WP Timetics was positioned as a general-purpose scheduling and booking plugin. That's accurate but unhelpful for search: "scheduling plugin" competes against hundreds of products. Fitness studios, gyms, and personal trainers searching for class booking solutions were using vertical-specific queries ("gym scheduling plugin", "fitness class booking WordPress") that WP Timetics had no content for.

The opportunity: fitness businesses have specific booking needs (class schedules, recurring sessions, trainer assignment, capacity limits) that generic scheduling content doesn't address. Writing specifically to this vertical could capture traffic that broad-positioning competitors weren't targeting.

Strategy 02 / 04

Built a four-piece content sequence that moved from educational breadth to product-specific conversion:

  • 101 Guide: A comprehensive guide to scheduling and managing fitness classes using WP Timetics. Top-of-funnel, keyword-led, establishing the product as a credible option in the fitness space.
  • Benefits piece: A focused document on the specific benefits of WP Timetics for fitness businesses, moving from feature descriptions to outcome language ("manage 50 concurrent class bookings" rather than "supports multiple events").
  • Quick outline: A structural outline for "Why WP Timetics is the Best Choice for Your WordPress Fitness Website," mapping the conversion-stage argument.
  • Full blog draft: The complete post, built from the outline, targeting bottom-of-funnel comparison queries.

Execution 03 / 04

  • "WP Timetics 101: A Comprehensive Guide to Scheduling and Managing Fitness Classes" (full blog).
  • "Experience the Power of WP Timetics for Your Fitness Business" (benefits-led content).
  • Content outline for the conversion-stage piece, mapping argument flow before drafting.
  • "Why WP Timetics is the Best Choice for Your WordPress Fitness Website" (full blog draft).
  • Supporting benefits document providing the feature-to-outcome translations used across all four pieces.
