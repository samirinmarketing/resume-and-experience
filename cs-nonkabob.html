Non-Kabob Dhaka

Communicating a Turkish-Bengali fusion identity on Facebook — and driving family footfall to two Dhaka locations through food storytelling.

The Problem 01 / 04

Non-Kabob is a Turkish-Bengali fusion kebab brand operating across Banasree and Aftabnagar — two dense, family-oriented neighbourhoods in east Dhaka. The brand's core proposition — Turkish grilling heritage meets Bengali palate — was genuinely distinctive, but their Facebook content wasn't translating that story into appetite or footfall.

Posts leaned on generic food photography without copy that explained the "why" — why Turkish, why Bengali fusion, why Banasree. The result was content that blended into the crowded Dhaka food-brand feed rather than standing out.

Strategy 02 / 04

Led with the fusion story. Most Dhaka food brands compete on price or proximity; Non-Kabob's edge was its concept, so the content strategy anchored every post to the Turkish-meets-Bengali narrative.

  • Wrote copy that foregrounded the Turkish cooking technique (open-flame grilling, slow-marinated meats) and then localised the experience ("flavours your family already loves, cooked the way they've never tasted").
  • Targeted copy at Banasree and Aftabnagar families specifically — hyper-local references to build neighbourhood brand affinity.
  • Developed a "breakfast, lunch, dine-out" content rhythm to communicate that Non-Kabob serves all three dayparts — an underutilised differentiator.

Execution 03 / 04

  • Facebook caption copy for signature dish spotlights, foregrounding the Turkish heritage angle.
  • Video scripts for short-form content showing the open-flame cooking process — the most shareable visual hook the brand had.
  • Location-specific copy callouts for Banasree and Aftabnagar audiences to reinforce neighbourhood presence.
  • Occasion-based posts (Eid, family gathering prompts) with dine-out CTAs tailored to the 4–8 person group dining context.
