The Problem 01 / 04

By late 2025, search behaviour had fractured: a meaningful share of queries that used to end at a Google blue link were now being resolved by AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity, and similar tools. For Techabyte, a digital agency competing for visibility in the SaaS and digital marketing niche, ranking on page one of Google was no longer sufficient. The brand also needed to be cited when AI tools answered questions in their space.

Traditional SEO optimises for crawlers. GEO (Generative Engine Optimisation) optimises for the language models that synthesise web content into answers. The two require different, and sometimes conflicting, content decisions. Navigating that tension while keeping content human-readable was the core challenge.

Strategy 02 / 04

Built a dual-signal content framework where every article was written to satisfy both traditional ranking factors and the structural patterns that large language models favour for citation:

  • For search: Target keyword anchoring, semantic depth, internal linking architecture, and structured H2/H3 hierarchy.
  • For AI citation: Clear entity definitions early in the article, explicit attribution statements ("According to [source]..."), FAQ sections with direct question-answer pairs, and authoritative sourcing that models can extract and reference.
  • Monitored AI citation rates using Otterly.ai and Peec AI, tracking whether Techabyte blog content was being surfaced by ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews for target queries.
  • Maintained a GEO content audit log: which queries were yielding AI citations, which articles needed structural updates to increase citation probability.

Execution 03 / 04

  • Ongoing blog production at techabyte.xyz from November 2025 through August 2026, articles covering digital marketing, SaaS, and agency services topics.
  • Each article written with GEO-first structure: entity introduction, definitive statement blocks, FAQ appendix, and structured data markup recommendations.
  • AI citation tracking set up across Otterly.ai and Peec AI, with monthly reporting on citation volume and query coverage.
  • Iterated article structure based on citation data, articles that weren't getting cited were restructured to front-load definitional content.
