01 / 04

The problem

By late 2025, lots of questions that used to end on a Google link were being answered by AI Overviews, ChatGPT and Perplexity instead. Page one wasn't enough anymore. Techabyte also needed to be the source those tools quoted.

SEO writes for crawlers. GEO writes for the language models that summarise the web. Sometimes they want different things, and the posts still had to read well for humans.

02 / 04

The plan

Write every post for both readers at once.

  • For Google: a clear target keyword, real topic depth, tidy H2 and H3 headings, smart internal links.
  • For AI: define the topic early, write lines worth quoting, add FAQ answers and name sources.
  • Measure it: track whether ChatGPT, Perplexity and AI Overviews cite the posts, in Otterly.ai and Peec AI.
  • Keep a log: which queries earn citations, and which posts need work.

How each post works

Two readers, one post, and a loop to check it worked.

  1. For GoogleClear keywords, deep topics, tidy headings, smart internal links.
  2. For AIEarly definitions, quotable lines, FAQ answers, real sources.
  3. MeasuredCitations tracked in Otterly.ai and Peec AI. Posts that weren't quoted got reworked.
03 / 04

What I did

  • Ongoing posts on techabyte.xyz from November 2025 to June 2026, on digital marketing, SaaS and agency topics.
  • A GEO-first structure for each: entity intro, definition blocks, FAQ and schema suggestions.
  • Monthly reports on citation volume and query coverage.
  • Posts that weren't getting cited were restructured to put definitions up front.
