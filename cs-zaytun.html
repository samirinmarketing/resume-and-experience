Case Study

Zaytun Restaurant & Convention Hall

Building a Bengali–English social media voice for Dhaka's garden rooftop dining brand — and turning Facebook into a booking engine for weddings and events.

ClientZaytun Restaurant & Convention Hall
PlatformFacebook
TypeSocial Media Marketing
LocationAgargaon, Dhaka
Social Media CopywritingBilingual Copy (Bangla/English)Video ScriptingContent CalendarOccasion Marketing

The Problem 01 / 04

Zaytun is a Garden & Rooftop Fine-Dining Restaurant in Agargaon, Dhaka, specialising in wedding, holud, and corporate event hosting. Despite a premium offering, their Facebook presence was inconsistent — posts went up sporadically, lacked a unified tone, and weren't converting social reach into event enquiries.

The challenge was dual-channel: Dhaka's food audience expects Bangla-first content with an emotional, occasion-led hook, while event planners (the highest-value buyers) respond to structured, benefit-led copy in English. Neither was being served well.

Strategy 02 / 04

Mapped Zaytun's content calendar against Dhaka's major occasion calendar — Eid, Pohela Boishakh, Mother's Day, wedding season peaks — to ensure every post was timed to a booking decision window.

  • Developed a bilingual (Bangla + English) voice guide: warm and celebratory in Bangla for organic reach; professional and persuasive in English for event package promotion.
  • Created three recurring content formats: occasion greetings with food storytelling, event package spotlights with soft CTAs, and behind-the-scenes rooftop ambience reels.
  • Scripted short-form video content for Facebook Reels to showcase the rooftop setting — the brand's single strongest visual asset.

Execution 03 / 04

Delivered an ongoing content programme across Facebook including:

  • Bilingual caption copy for food and ambience posts — Bangla headline, English body, branded hashtags.
  • Event package promotion copy emphasising the "all-in-one" hosting angle (venue + catering + decor coordination).
  • Occasion-led posts for Eid, Puja, and wedding season with emotional, shareable copy.
  • Video scripts for rooftop and garden showcase reels, structured as a visual walk-through to drive "save" and "share" behaviours.
