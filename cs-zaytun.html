Zaytun Restaurant & Convention Hall · Social media

Bilingual social media for a Dhaka rooftop restaurant

Zaytun hosts weddings, holud nights and iftars on a garden rooftop in Agargaon. Its Facebook page posted now and then, in no particular voice. I gave it one voice, in two languages.

At a glance

  • One Bangla and English voice: warm Bangla for reach, clear English for event packages.
  • Posts timed to Eid, Pohela Boishakh and wedding season, when people actually book.
  • Three repeating post types, plus reel scripts built around the rooftop.

01 / 04

The problem

Great venue, quiet page. Posts went up whenever, with no shared tone, and they weren't turning likes into event enquiries.

Two audiences made it trickier. Food lovers in Dhaka scroll in Bangla and want feeling. Event planners, the big-ticket buyers, want clear facts in English. Nobody was talking to either properly.

02 / 04

The plan

Line the calendar up with the moments people book: Eid, Pohela Boishakh, Mother's Day and wedding season.

  • A voice guide for both languages: celebratory in Bangla, persuasive in English.
  • Three recurring formats: occasion posts with food stories, event package spotlights with soft calls to action, and rooftop reels.
  • Reels scripted around the rooftop, the brand's best-looking asset.
03 / 04

What I did

  • Bilingual captions: Bangla headline, English body, branded hashtags.
  • Event package copy around the all-in-one angle: venue, catering and decor in one place.
  • Occasion posts for Eid, Puja and wedding season.
  • Walk-through scripts for rooftop and garden reels, written to earn saves and shares.
