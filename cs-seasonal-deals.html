01 / 04

The problem

Arraytics and ThemeWinter run Timetics, Eventin and WPCafe. Every sale meant new deal pages, new emails and new affiliate coordination, all from a blank page.

That meant mismatched brands, and the same problems solved again every quarter.

02 / 04

The plan

Build once, reuse every season.

  • Deal page template: swap the brand voice and product details, keep the layout, urgency and calls to action.
  • Affiliate email template: drop in campaign details and send to the partner network.
  • In-plugin ads: the deal shows up inside all three plugins, right in the WordPress dashboard.
03 / 04

What I did

  • Black Friday deal pages for ThemeWinter (Oct 2023) and Arraytics (Nov 2023), on one template.
  • Arraytics Holiday sale page for December.
  • Ramadan deal pages for both brands (2024).
  • Black Friday affiliate email.
  • In-plugin ad copy for Eventin, WPCafe and Timetics.
