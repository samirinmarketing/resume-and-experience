A Seasonal Deal Campaign System

Not five one-off campaigns. One repeatable system, run across two brands (Arraytics and ThemeWinter) for Black Friday, Holiday, Ramadan, Mother Language Day, and Summer sale cycles.

The Problem 01 / 04

Arraytics and ThemeWinter run multiple WordPress plugins (Timetics, Eventin, WPCafe) under two parent brands. Each brand needed deal pages, email campaigns, and promotional assets for every seasonal window: Black Friday, Holiday season, Ramadan, and Summer sales.

Without a system, each campaign was being built from scratch: new copy, new layout, new affiliate coordination, every time. This created inconsistency across brands and wasted time re-solving problems that had already been solved the previous quarter.

Strategy 02 / 04

Designed a campaign template system that could be instantiated for any seasonal window with minimal customisation:

  • A deal-page copy structure that could be rebranded between Arraytics and ThemeWinter by swapping product details and brand voice, while keeping the layout, urgency mechanics, and CTA structure consistent.
  • An affiliate email template for Black Friday coordination, enabling outreach to the existing affiliate network with campaign-specific details slotted into a proven structure.
  • In-plugin ad copy spanning all three plugins (Eventin, WPCafe, Timetics WP), ensuring users of any ThemeWinter product saw the seasonal promotion without leaving their WordPress dashboard.

Execution 03 / 04

  • ThemeWinter Black Friday Deal Page Copy (Oct 2023) and Arraytics Black Friday Deal Page Copy (Nov 2023), as separate documents following the same structural template.
  • Arraytics Holiday Sales Deal Page Copy for the December window.
  • Ramadan Deal Page Copy for both Arraytics and ThemeWinter (2024).
  • Black Friday affiliate email copy for outreach to the existing partner network.
  • In-plugin ad copy for Eventin, WPCafe, and Timetics WP, cross-promoting deals within the product UI.
