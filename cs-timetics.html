01 / 04

The problem

Timetics.ai is an AI scheduling tool for online and offline meetings and events: seat plans, smart recommendations, attendee management and more.

Product Hunt voters are founders, developers and early adopters. They upvote insight, not slogans.

AppSumo buyers are small business owners who've seen a hundred SaaS pitches. They buy on clarity, proof and deal value.

02 / 04

The plan

Two separate briefs, two voices.

  • Product Hunt: open with the insight, "every event planner is planning for chaos, not success", then the fix, then quick outcome bullets.
  • AppSumo: start with the planner's frustration, flip it with "what if your scheduling tool thought ahead", then make the lifetime deal value obvious.
  • Kenmoo and MarTech: shorter versions adapted from the AppSumo page.
  • Launch day: reply templates to keep the maker active while the ranking window was open.
03 / 04

What I did

  • Product Hunt: headline, tagline, description and maker comment.
  • AppSumo: the full deal page, from problem to use cases to deal breakdown.
  • Replies to community questions and reviews during launch.
  • Distribution copy for Kenmoo and MarTech.
