Case Study

Timetics.ai

GTM copy strategy for the dual-platform launch of an AI-powered scheduling tool, Product Hunt for community traction, AppSumo for revenue.

The Problem 01 / 04

Timetics is an AI-powered scheduling tool for online and offline meetings and events, seat arrangements, personalized recommendations, attendee management, and more. Arraytics needed to launch it on two very different platforms with very different audiences and conversion mechanics:

Product Hunt is a maker-community platform where upvotes come from founders, developers, and early adopters who are allergic to marketing speak. Copy needs to be honest, punchy, and demonstrate genuine insight into the problem space.

AppSumo is a deal platform. The audience is pragmatic, small business owners who've seen hundreds of SaaS pitches and buy on clarity, social proof, and deal value, not feature lists. The copy needed to earn trust before it sold anything.

Strategy 02 / 04

Wrote both listings as completely separate briefs with different voice, structure, and hierarchy of information:

  • Product Hunt: Led with the problem insight ("every event planner is planning for chaos, not success"), introduced Timetics as the specific solution, kept feature bullets scannable and outcome-oriented.
  • AppSumo: Opened with the shared frustration of event planners (relatable pain, not product), moved to the contrast ("what if your scheduling tool thought ahead"), then introduced Timetics. Deal copy emphasised the LTD value clearly.
  • Supported distribution across Kenmoo and MarTech social marketplaces with adapted short-form copy.
  • Wrote post-launch comment response templates to maintain maker presence and engagement on Product Hunt during the ranking window.

Execution 03 / 04

  • Product Hunt listing: headline, tagline, product description, and maker comment copy.
  • AppSumo deal page: full sales copy covering the problem, product intro, key features, use cases, and deal breakdown.
  • Post-launch engagement copy: responses to community questions and reviews during the live ranking period.
  • Platform distribution copy for Kenmoo and MarTech adapted from the AppSumo page.
