Artmap Argyll · Social media · Scotland

Bringing new visitors to Open Studios in Argyll

Artmap Argyll is a network of artists on Scotland's west coast. Every year they open their studios to the public. Locals knew about it. Almost nobody else did.

At a glance

  • Written from the artists' side: sell the visit, not the event.
  • Place-led language rooted in Argyll's coast and landscape.
  • Artist features, reel scripts and posts that invite comments and nominations.

01 / 04

The problem

Arts groups are tricky to write for. The copy had to feel warm to existing supporters and still welcome people who'd never been to an arts event.

A plain "come to our event" wasn't going to work for an audience that values the real thing.

02 / 04

The plan

Sell the experience, not the listing: stepping into a working studio, chatting with the maker, taking home something truly handmade.

  • Name the feeling of Open Studios, not just the dates.
  • Use the west coast and its landscape, so it can't be mistaken for any other arts fair.
  • Open every reel with a scene, then give the details.
03 / 04

What I did

  • Facebook captions for the Open Studios 2025 series.
  • Artist feature posts introducing members and their work.
  • Reel scripts for event promos and the call for artists.
  • Community posts built to get comments and artist self-nominations.
