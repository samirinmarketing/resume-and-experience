Artmap Argyll

Social media copywriting for a Scottish artists' network — promoting Open Studios events and connecting Argyll's creative community to new audiences.

The Problem 01 / 04

Artmap Argyll is a network of artists living and working on the west coast of Scotland. Their primary annual event — Open Studios — invites the public into working artist spaces across Argyll, but awareness outside the immediate community was limited.

The challenge: arts organisations are notoriously hard to write for at scale. The copy needed to feel warm and community-rooted (for existing supporters) while being accessible enough to pull in new visitors who weren't already part of the arts scene. Generic "come to our event" copy wouldn't work for an audience that values authenticity.

Strategy 02 / 04

Wrote from the artists' perspective rather than the organisation's. Instead of promoting the event, the copy promoted the experience of visiting a working studio — the serendipity of it, the directness of talking to the maker, the chance to buy something genuinely handmade.

  • Developed copy that named the feeling of Open Studios rather than just the logistics — positioning it as a cultural experience, not just an arts fair.
  • Used landscape and place-based language tied to Argyll's west coast setting to differentiate from generic arts event marketing.
  • Wrote video script copy for promotional reels that opened with a scene-setting visual hook before introducing the event details.

Execution 03 / 04

  • Facebook caption copy for Open Studios 2025 event promotion series.
  • Artist feature posts introducing individual Artmap members and their practice.
  • Video scripts for event promo reels and "call for artists" campaign content.
  • Community engagement copy — post formats designed to generate comments and artist self-nominations.
