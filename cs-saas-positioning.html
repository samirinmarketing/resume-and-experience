Case Study

WordPress Plugin to SaaS

Timetics existed as a WordPress plugin. Timetics.ai launched as a standalone SaaS. One product, two delivery models, two buyer mindsets, one brand that needed to hold both without confusing either.

The Problem 01 / 04

Timetics started as a WordPress plugin. When the SaaS version (Timetics.ai) launched, the positioning problem multiplied: the WordPress buyer wants self-hosted control, plugin ecosystem compatibility, and a one-time purchase. The SaaS buyer wants zero maintenance, hosted infrastructure, and predictable monthly pricing.

Both products share core scheduling functionality but differ in deployment, support model, and technical requirements. The landing page, the feature descriptions, and every comparison needed to speak to the right buyer without undermining the other product's value proposition.

Strategy 02 / 04

Built a structured framework to separate the two audiences cleanly:

  • Created a 20-row feature/deployment/support/technicality comparison matrix (SaaS vs. WP) to establish where the products genuinely diverge, not just where the pricing differs.
  • Developed a narrative structure for the SaaS landing page using story blocks rather than feature lists. The headline framework: "Timetics Lets You Create More Than Just a Booking Form," followed by four benefit-led blocks (Meaningful Meetings, Visual Seat Plan, Automation, Scale Without Limits).
  • Wrote a full SaaS landing page copy document with a connected Figma prototype for the design team.
  • Addressed SaaS-specific questions ("what happens to my data?", "can I use my own domain?") in a separate FAQ document rather than burying them in the landing page.

Execution 03 / 04

  • SaaS vs. WordPress comparison matrix covering features, deployment, support, and technical requirements.
  • SaaS landing page story blocks defining the narrative arc and benefit hierarchy.
  • Full SaaS landing page copy with Figma prototype link for design handoff.
  • SaaS roadmap content document covering planned features (Agency Accounts, AI Chatbot, Custom Domain, Email Notification).
  • SaaS questions-and-answers document for internal alignment and external FAQ.
  • Introductory blog post for the SaaS launch.
  • Updated privacy policy for the SaaS product.
