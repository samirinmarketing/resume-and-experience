One product, two ways to buy it

Timetics started as a WordPress plugin. Then Timetics.ai launched as a SaaS. Same core, two kinds of buyer, and one brand that had to make sense to both.

The problem

The WordPress buyer wants control, plugin compatibility and a one-time price. The SaaS buyer wants zero maintenance, hosting handled and a steady monthly bill.

Same scheduling core, different setup, support and tech. Every page had to talk to the right buyer without knocking the other product.

02 / 04

The plan

  • A 20-row matrix comparing features, deployment, support and technical needs, to find the real differences, not just the price ones.
  • A story-led landing page. Headline: "Timetics Lets You Create More Than Just a Booking Form". Then four benefit blocks: Meaningful Meetings, Visual Seat Plan, Automation, Scale Without Limits.
  • Full landing page copy, linked to a Figma prototype for design.
  • SaaS questions ("what happens to my data?", "can I use my own domain?") moved to their own FAQ.
03 / 04

What I did

  • SaaS vs WordPress comparison matrix.
  • Landing page story blocks and full copy, with the Figma handoff.
  • Roadmap content: agency accounts, AI chatbot, custom domain, email notifications.
  • A Q&A doc for the team and the public FAQ.
  • Launch blog post.
  • Updated privacy policy for the SaaS.
