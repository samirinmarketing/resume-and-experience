The Problem 01 / 04

Somalicious, a hand-crafted fashion brand positioning itself as "where style meets comfort", needed to establish a premium brand identity on social media that felt genuinely luxurious without alienating the brand's accessible, design-forward audience.

The challenge: luxury fashion content in Bangladesh often defaults to either Western aesthetic pastiche or generic product photography. Somalicious needed content that felt distinctly its own, editorial, aspirational, and culturally resonant, to justify premium pricing against a crowded fast-fashion market.

Strategy 02 / 04

Built the content strategy around Somalicious's core tension: handcraft meets contemporary style. This "made carefully, worn confidently" narrative became the throughline across every content format.

  • Developed brand copy guidelines covering tone of voice (confident, understated, poetic), preferred vocabulary, and content taboos.
  • Scripted and storyboarded video content to show the making process, hands, fabric, detail shots, building perceived value before showing the finished product.
  • Directed photography briefs that prioritised texture, light, and setting over face-forward model shots, to position the garments as the hero.

Execution 03 / 04

  • Brand copywriting: taglines, about copy, and product description templates reflecting the luxury-craft positioning.
  • Video scripts and storyboards for product launch reels and brand introduction content.
  • Social media caption copy for Facebook and Instagram, maintaining consistent editorial tone across occasions and product launches.
  • Photography direction notes for in-house shoots, background, lighting, styling, and prop guidance.
  • Interactive media design concepts for carousel and story formats to show product details and craftsmanship.
