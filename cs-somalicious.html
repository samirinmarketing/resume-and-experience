Somalicious · Brand · Social media

Brand voice and social content for a handcrafted fashion label

Somalicious makes handcrafted clothes in Dhaka. It wanted to feel premium on social without feeling out of reach. I built the voice first, then everything around it.

At a glance

  • One line holds it together: made carefully, worn confidently.
  • Voice guide, scripts, storyboards and photo briefs in one system.
  • Content for reels, carousels and stories on Facebook and Instagram.

01 / 04

The problem

Fashion content in Bangladesh often goes one of two ways: borrowed Western looks, or plain product shots. Neither says premium, and neither says Somalicious.

The brand needed something of its own: editorial, aspirational and rooted in local culture, to earn its price in a crowded fast-fashion market.

02 / 04

The plan

Lean into the brand's tension: handcraft meets modern style.

  • A voice guide: confident, understated, a little poetic. Plus words to use and words to skip.
  • Videos that show the making first (hands, fabric, detail), then the finished piece.
  • Photo briefs that put texture, light and setting ahead of faces, so the clothes are the hero.
03 / 04

What I did

  • Taglines, about copy and product description templates.
  • Scripts and storyboards for launch reels and brand intro videos.
  • Captions for Facebook and Instagram across launches and occasions.
  • Photo direction notes: background, light, styling and props.
  • Carousel and story concepts that show the craft up close.
