01 / 04

The problem

A&C Building Contractors do plumbing, heating, joinery, roofing, roughcasting and full renovations. Their site said all of that in generic builder-speak, with no place names and no service pages. Google had no reason to show them for "builders Lochgilphead".

Brickstone Heritage, formerly RJD Brickwork, had just rebranded. They needed copy that carried 20+ years of stonework into a brand new name.

02 / 04

The plan

  • Research what homeowners search when they're ready to hire: "building contractors Lochgilphead", "stonework Argyll", "roughcasting Scotland".
  • Audit both sites: titles, meta descriptions, headings and internal links.
  • Give each service its own copy block and keyword, instead of one big "what we do" page.
  • Set up GA4 and Search Console first, for a clean baseline.
  • Tidy both Google Business Profiles for the local map pack.
03 / 04

What I did

  • Rewrote both sites with Lochgilphead, Argyll and nearby areas woven into the service copy.
  • Six service blocks for A&C: plumbing, heating, joinery, roofing, roughcasting and renovation.
  • New about copy for Brickstone Heritage that leads with the 20-year story and carries the RJD name across.
  • Local trust signals: years in business, areas covered, happy customers.
  • GA4 and Search Console, with contact form submissions tracked as key events.
