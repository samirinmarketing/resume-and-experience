A&C Building Contractors & Brickstone Heritage

Full-funnel local SEO and web copy for two Lochgilphead construction businesses, improving search visibility and converting site visitors into enquiries.

The Problem 01 / 04

Two established building companies in Lochgilphead, Argyll were near-invisible in local search results despite years of trading and a strong local reputation:

A&C Building Contractors offered a comprehensive range of services (plumbing, heating, joinery, roofing, roughcasting, full renovations) but their website copy buried the offer in generic contractor language, no location signals, no service-specific pages, no reason for Google to surface them for "builders Lochgilphead" queries.

Brickstone Heritage (formerly RJD Brickwork) had recently rebranded and needed web copy that reflected their 20+ year heritage in stonework and masonry while establishing the new brand name in local search.

Strategy 02 / 04

Ran keyword research targeting homeowner-intent search queries in the Lochgilphead and Argyll area, prioritising transactional terms ("building contractors Lochgilphead", "stonework Argyll", "roughcasting Scotland") over informational content at this stage.

  • Audited both sites for on-page SEO signals: title tags, meta descriptions, header structure, internal linking, and keyword density.
  • Mapped services to search intent, each core service needed its own copy block with a specific keyword anchor, not one merged "what we do" page.
  • Set up Google Analytics 4 and Google Search Console for both clients and established baseline tracking before any copy changes went live.
  • Optimised Google My Business profiles for both companies to strengthen local pack rankings.

Execution 03 / 04

  • Rewrote website copy for both clients with location signals embedded naturally, Lochgilphead, Argyll, and surrounding areas named throughout service descriptions.
  • Created service-specific copy blocks for A&C (plumbing, heating, joinery, roofing, roughcasting, renovation), each with distinct keyword anchoring.
  • Wrote Brickstone Heritage's new brand about copy, leading with the 20-year heritage story and translating "RJD Brickwork" equity into the new name.
  • Added social proof language and local trust signals (years in business, area coverage, customer satisfaction emphasis) to support conversion.
  • Set up GA4 + Search Console tracking and configured key event tracking for contact form submissions.
