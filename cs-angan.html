Angan Properties

A series of data-rich, search-optimised real estate blog posts targeting high-intent flat price queries for Dhaka's premium residential neighbourhoods.

The Problem 01 / 04

Angan Properties needed to capture organic search traffic from Bangladeshi homebuyers and investors researching flat prices in Dhaka's most sought-after areas — Dhanmondi, Gulshan, and Mirpur. These are high commercial-intent queries: people searching "flat price in Dhanmondi" are typically close to a buying or renting decision.

The challenge: real estate content in Bangladesh is often thin, agency-generic, and lacking the specific price data that search users actually need. To rank and convert, Angan needed content that answered the question precisely — with real numbers, area-specific context, and investment rationale — not just a generic "contact us for pricing" deflection.

Strategy 02 / 04

Structured each article around a specific neighbourhood with its own keyword anchor ("flat price in Dhanmondi", "flat cost in Gulshan", "flat price in Mirpur") and built the content to serve both search intent and the reader's actual decision-making process:

  • Led with a direct price range answer in the opening paragraph — Google favours pages that answer the query immediately for featured snippet capture.
  • Segmented prices by flat size (1,000 sq ft, 1,200 sq ft) and price tier (standard, premium, lake-facing) to match the range of search sub-intents within each query.
  • Added neighbourhood-specific context (metro rail access, school proximity, developer reputation tiers) to justify price differentials and extend time-on-page.
  • Structured with H2 and H3 sections matching predicted follow-up queries ("why is Gulshan more expensive?", "is Mirpur a good investment?")

Execution 03 / 04

  • "What Is the Flat Price in Dhanmondi in 2026?" — Covered the BDT 14,000–30,000/sqft range, road-by-road price breakdown, premium lake-facing premiums on Roads 8A and 11A.
  • "How Much Does a Flat Cost in Gulshan? Updated 2026 Price Guide" — BDT 17,000–28,000/sqft range, luxury positioning rationale, investor angle.
  • "What Is the Flat Price in Mirpur, Dhaka (2026)?" — BDT 6,000–10,000/sqft range, metro rail connectivity as a value driver, budget-buyer framing.
