What does a flat in Dhaka really cost?

Three price guides for Angan Properties, one each for Dhanmondi, Gulshan and Mirpur. Written for people about to buy, and built to win Google's featured snippets.

At a glance

  • Each guide targets one area's price search, like "flat price in Dhanmondi".
  • The price range comes first, in the opening lines, where snippets are pulled from.
  • Prices split by flat size and tier, with the reasons behind the gaps.

01 / 04

The problem

People searching "flat price in Dhanmondi" are close to buying. They want numbers.

Most real estate content in Bangladesh gives them "contact us for pricing" instead. Thin pages like that don't rank, and they don't help anyone decide.

02 / 04

The plan

One area per article, one keyword per area. Then answer the question straight away.

  • Put the price range in the first paragraph. Google likes pages that answer fast.
  • Split prices by size (1,000 and 1,200 sq ft) and tier (standard, premium, lake-facing).
  • Explain the gaps: metro rail, schools, developer reputation.
  • Use headings that match the next question, like "Is Mirpur a good investment?"
03 / 04

What I did

  • Dhanmondi: BDT 14,000–30,000 per sq ft, road by road, with the lake-facing premium on Roads 8A and 11A.
  • Gulshan: BDT 17,000–28,000 per sq ft, the luxury case and the investor angle.
  • Mirpur: BDT 6,000–10,000 per sq ft, with metro rail as the value driver for budget buyers.
